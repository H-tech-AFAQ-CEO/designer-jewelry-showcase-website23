# Atelier North

## Developed by Afaq Ahmad

Atelier North is a premium editorial website for quietly considered jewellery, bespoke watches, and leather straps made by hand in London.

## What is complete

- Premium editorial homepage with hero storytelling and featured collections
- Four dedicated pages with their own sections and navigation:
  - Collection: jewellery, watches, and leather straps
  - Atelier: craft, materials, and studio process
  - Journal: editorial stories and notes from the atelier
  - Contact: bespoke enquiries and studio information
- Refined luxury visual system with Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans typography
- Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile screens
- Warm ivory, espresso, and muted brass palette with editorial spacing
- Local imagery for the hero and collection sections
- Accessible navigation, semantic sections, and responsive controls
- Default v0/Vercel favicon assets removed

## Recommended next steps

- Connect the contact form to a real email or CRM workflow
- Replace mock imagery with final commissioned product photography
- Add product detail pages, filtering, and a working enquiry flow
- Add CMS-managed journal content and collection inventory
- Configure a custom domain, analytics goals, and SEO/social sharing images
- Add legal pages such as privacy, terms, and shipping/returns when commerce is enabled
